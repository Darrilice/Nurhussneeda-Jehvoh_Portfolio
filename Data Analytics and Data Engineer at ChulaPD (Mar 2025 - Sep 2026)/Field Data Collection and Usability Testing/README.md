# Field Data Collection, Usability Testing and UX Requirements

I supported multimodal CheckPD field collection across provinces. Sessions covered voice, tremor, finger tapping, gait, balance and risk questionnaires. Participants came through self-initiated use, hospitals and field activities.

I tested the workflow and shared feedback on barriers for older users. Changes included a clearer interface, removal of tests that some users found difficult and spoken instructions. Direct observations from field staff helped explain partial and unstarted screenings in dashboard reviews.

I also gathered requirements from nutrition, exercise and sleep teams and worked with UX/UI designers to turn them into app flows. For reporting, I gathered requirements from executives and coordinators and refined dashboard views around their questions about usage, completion and assessment scores.
