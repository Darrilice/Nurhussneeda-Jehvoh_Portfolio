# Research and Publication

I co-authored [National implementation of a digital Parkinson’s disease screening programme in Thailand: reach, adoption, and real-world performance of the CheckPD app](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12889-026-27654-0), published in *BMC Public Health* on 8 May 2026.

Project materials include dataset preparation, demographic encoding, tables, figures and workflow diagrams that supported the manuscript. In the published study, 13,381 of 18,520 users completed screening across 10 provinces. Among 730 screen-positive participants evaluated by neurologists, 593 had a confirmed diagnosis, corresponding to a positive predictive value of 81.23% for that evaluated group. These are published programme-level findings.
