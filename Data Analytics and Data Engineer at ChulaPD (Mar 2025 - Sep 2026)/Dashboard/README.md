# CheckPD and Screening Assessments Dashboards

I gathered reporting requirements from executives and project coordinators, then built, maintained and revised six Google Data Studio (Looker Studio) dashboards. Python supported data preparation and analysis. All pages were completed and used by the project team.

## CheckPD reporting

| Reporting area | What the dashboard helped the team do |
| --- | --- |
| App reach and usage | Track distinct users, compare changes by province and identify periods of heavier usage. |
| Screening completion | Compare users who completed all, some or none of the required tests. Identify which tests were most and least often completed among partial users. |
| Before and after the app update | Compare user reach and each completion category across the two periods. The required test battery changed with the update, so completion refers to the tests required in each period. |
| Risk screening and physician follow-up | Compare app risk classifications with physician outcomes for both risk groups where follow-up was available. Keep records without a follow-up result separate from the physician outcome categories. |

## Problem addressed and observed outcome

The project team needed to understand where users stopped and why. The completion breakdown showed which tests users tended to leave unfinished. Direct observations from field staff helped the team interpret the patterns, including health limitations, difficult tests for older users and steps that were hard to follow.

Executives asked whether the revised app helped more users finish screening. The release simplified parts of the interface, removed tests that some older users found difficult and added spoken instructions for users who had difficulty reading. The dashboard showed that the share of users completing the required tests increased after the update. A verified percentage and the direction of change for the partial and unstarted groups are unavailable for this portfolio. A complete session also gave downstream analysts a set of measurements collected under more comparable conditions within one attempt.

## Screening Assessments reporting

The dashboard displayed score distributions for the PD, prodromal and control groups across cognition, mood, motor symptoms, sleep, smell, constipation, diet and color vision. The measures covered MoCA, TMSE, HAM-D, MDS-UPDRS, Epworth Sleepiness Scale, Thai Smell Identification Test, RBD Questionnaire, ROME IV, Thai MIND Diet Assessment and Farnsworth D-15 Color Vision. The views supported exploratory comparisons. Diagnostic performance and group-separation conclusions were outside the analysis presented here.

Dashboard screenshots and internal figures are withheld because the dashboards contain personal and clinical information.
